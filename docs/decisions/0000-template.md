# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owners:** Name or team
- **Supersedes:** None

## Context

Describe the forces, constraints, evidence, and problem. Distinguish verified
facts from assumptions.

## Decision

State the decision directly and define its scope.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier or safer?

### Negative

- What cost, limitation, or follow-up work does this create?

## Alternatives considered

- Alternative and the reason it was not selected.

## Validation

List benchmarks, prototypes, tests, or production evidence required to confirm
the decision. If validation fails, return the ADR to Proposed or supersede it.
